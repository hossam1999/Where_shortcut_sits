\paragraph{What carries the shortcut decides the method} Across six real and controlled settings three regimes
emerge. (1) \emph{Distinct overlays that carry the shortcut in their pixels} (sonographer calipers, synthetic
rulers, debris and tubes): masking removes an out-of-ROI copy but leaves an in-ROI copy intact, and U-MtE removes
most of what masking leaves (thyroid $+0.22$, $+0.20$, $+0.13$ on three backbones; controlled sweeps $+0.08$ to
$+0.28$). (2) \emph{Artifacts that resemble the pathology} (capsule debris versus erosion fibrin): any subspace
estimated from artifact appearance removes disease evidence; disease protection makes erasure safe, and with
image-level artifact labels masking plus last-layer retraining is the most robust option. (3) \emph{Artifacts
whose shortcut is carried by correlates} (dermoscopic hair co-varies with body site, age and sex; a chest drain
comes with a treated, re-expanded lung): removing even every artifact patch with an oracle mask gains little
($+0.015$ for hair), no pixel- or insertion-based method can close the gap, and label-based group methods (DFR,
balancing, JTT on hair) are preferable. The oracle-removal ceiling is a cheap diagnostic of the regime.

\paragraph{Why erasure works where masking and patch removal do not} In thyroid ultrasound, dropping every patch
token that contains a caliper (oracle masks) before mean-pooling improves the reversed AUROC by only $+0.047$
over pooling all ROI tokens, whereas erasing the generic-overlay subspace from the class-token representation
improves on masking by $+0.22$ (the two representations differ, so the comparison is indicative). Self-attention distributes the
information about a salient overlay across the whole representation; an in-ROI artifact is therefore not
contained in ``its'' pixels or patches, and a representation-level operation is needed to remove it.

\paragraph{Erasure, not augmentation} The same overlays used as training augmentation gain at most $+0.03$ over
masking in every cohort; used to estimate and erase a subspace they gain up to $+0.21$ more. Augmentation teaches
invariance to the \emph{generated} overlays; erasure removes the directions along which \emph{any} overlay moves
the representation, which transfers to real artifacts the generator never produced.

\paragraph{Reading reversed-environment gains} A reversed-test gain can be manufactured by removing
diagnosis-correlated signal together with the artifact: erasure fitted on unpaired image-level labels and post-hoc
prevalence recalibration invert the correlated/reversed ordering. We report reversed, correlated and clean AUROC
for every method and summarise robustness by the minimum of correlated and reversed AUROC.

\paragraph{Decision rule for practitioners} (1) Measure where artifacts sit relative to the ROI; out-of-ROI
artifacts are removed by masking. (2) For in-ROI artifacts without any artifact annotation, use protected U-MtE;
it matches oracle-mask LaMa inpainting where the artifact is a distinct overlay. (3) With image-level artifact
labels, combine masking or U-MtE with group balancing or last-layer retraining, choosing among them on a group-aware
validation split; prefer label-based reweighting alone when the shortcut is carried by correlates of the artifact,
which the oracle-removal ceiling (a handful of annotated masks) reveals.

\paragraph{Limitations}
(i) Backbones are frozen and heads linear; an end-to-end fine-tuning check is reported but not a full sweep.
(ii) Artifact masks for thyroid calipers and capsule debris come from an audited rule-based detector
(precision/recall 0.86--0.89) and a patch-token probe (IoU 0.62); errors act as label noise on $A$ and on the
overlap strata. (iii) Hair and ruler share one annotation channel in the ISIC 2019 archive. (iv) No patient or
video identifiers are published for TN3K or SEE-AI; leakage groups use perceptual hashes and frame blocks.
(v) Chest-drain labels for pneumothorax-negative radiographs come from a detector. (vi) The controlled chest-X-ray
sweep did not show the sign reversal: the encoder ignored extra-pulmonary tubes. (vii) Reversed environments are
constructed by subsampling; real deployment shifts are less extreme, and the clean results bound the cost without
shift. (viii) The disease-protection step needs diagnosis labels of artifact-free images, and the balanced variants
need image-level artifact labels.
