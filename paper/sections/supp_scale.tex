\section*{S7. Backbone scale}
Replacing DINOv2 ViT-B/14 (86\,M parameters) by ViT-S/14 (21\,M) or ViT-L/14 (304\,M), with everything else fixed
(pre-registered), the location crossover is positive in all nine cohort--size combinations (ViT-S / ViT-B / ViT-L:
thyroid $+0.244$ / $+0.230$ / $+0.271$; ovary $+0.298$ / $+0.170$ / $+0.202$; capsule $+0.370$ / $+0.368$ /
$+0.408$; every CI excludes zero), and protected U-MtE beats masking on thyroid and ovary at every size (ViT-S
$+0.181$ [$+0.146, +0.218$] and $+0.103$ [$+0.078, +0.128$]; ViT-L $+0.222$ [$+0.195, +0.256$] and $+0.060$
[$+0.043, +0.077$]). Larger models exploit the in-ROI shortcut \emph{more}: the ERM correlated--reversed gap grows
from 0.547 to 0.621 (thyroid) and from 0.319 to 0.570 (ovary) between ViT-S and ViT-L, so scale does not remove it.
On capsule, disease protection is less complete with ViT-L ($-0.040$ [$-0.046, -0.035$] versus masking).
