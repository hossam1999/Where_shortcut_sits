\section{Data}\label{sec:data}
\paragraph{Controlled dermoscopy cohort} ISIC 2018 Task 1/2 images with manual lesion masks and melanoma labels
from the union of the ISIC 2016 and 2017 tables (three images with conflicting labels dropped); perceptual-hash
(Hamming $\le 8$) and lesion grouping; 2,437 images for which a $65\times19$\,px ruler (0.46\% of a
$518\times518$ image) can be placed at 0/25/50/75/100\% overlap with the lesion within 10 percentage points
(train/val/test 1,849/371/371 before common support).

\paragraph{Real hair cohort} All 25,331 ISIC 2019 training images. Artifact masks for hair and rulers (one
channel, semi-automatic), ink and vignetting come from the archive of \citet{wegley2026artifacts}. Lesion masks are
manual for HAM10000 \citep{tschandl2018ham} (10,015) and ISIC 2018 Task 1 (2,593) images and predicted elsewhere
(12,723) by a U-Net (ResNet-34 encoder) trained on those manual masks (held-out Dice 0.939; 0.948 against manual
masks on held-out ISIC 2019 images). Leakage groups join lesion identifiers and pHash Hamming $\le 2$ (the pilot's
$\le 8$ chains unrelated dermoscopy images into a single component of 10,974 images at this scale). After quality
control (lesion area 0.5--95\%, no ink), images are hair-free (hair/ruler fraction $<0.001$; 2,287), Trap~A
(hair fraction $\ge 0.005$ and $r\ge0.5$; 5,095), Trap~B ($r<0.1$; 4,486) or template donors
($0.1\le r<0.5$; 8,104), which are used only by \ItoE{} and never trained or tested on. Swapping the lesion-mask
source (manual vs.\ U-Net) changes the stratum of 6.9\% of held-out hair images and never moves an image between
Trap~A and Trap~B.

\paragraph{Chest radiography} NIH ChestX-ray14 \citep{wang2017chestxray8}. Lung ROIs are the union of both lungs
from the TorchXRayVision PSPNet \citep{cohen2022xrv}. \emph{Controlled cohort:} 1,906 pneumothorax images without a
chest drain (NEATX annotations) and 5,718 \emph{No Finding} images from other patients, patient-grouped 60/20/20;
a $100\times12$\,px radio-opaque tube (0.45\% of the image) is placed at controlled overlap with the lung ROI
(7,610 images in common support). \emph{Real drains:} NEATX provides two non-expert drain annotations for 3,543
pneumothorax images (1,637 with a drain); drains on pneumothorax-negative images are labelled by a drain detector
trained on NEATX (Sec.~\ref{sec:drain_detector}). Environments are built within view position (AP/PA), the
radiographic analogue of image source.
