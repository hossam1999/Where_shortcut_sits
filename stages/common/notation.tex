\section*{Notation}
\begin{tabular}{p{3.2cm}p{11.8cm}}
$Y\in\{0,1\}$ & diagnosis (1 = melanoma / malignant / solid-component tumour / erosion). \\
$A\in\{0,1\}$ & artifact present (hair, sonographer caliper, capsule debris, chest tube). \\
ROI & region of interest (lesion, nodule, tumour, lesion box, lungs); drawn in \textcolor{roigreen}{green}; artifacts in \textcolor{artred}{red}. \\
$r$ & overlap $|\text{artifact}\cap\text{ROI}|/|\text{artifact}|$. Trap~A: $r\ge0.5$ (in-ROI); Trap~B: $r<0.1$ (out-of-ROI). \\
Environments & training and correlated test $P(A{=}1\mid Y{=}1)=0.9$, $P(A{=}1\mid Y{=}0)=0.1$; reversed test 0.1/0.9; clean test: artifact independent of $Y$ (real traps: half of each class; sweeps: absent). \\
ERM, mask & linear head on the frozen features of the original image / of the ROI-masked image. \\
Crossover & $[\text{mask}-\text{ERM}]_{\text{out}}-[\text{mask}-\text{ERM}]_{\text{in}}$ of reversed-test AUROC (real traps: Trap~B $-$ Trap~A; sweeps and transplant: location interaction on the same images). \\
CI & 95\% crossed seed$\times$image bootstrap interval (Stage~1, Sec.~\ref{sec:bootstrap}); every interval in these reports uses it. \\
$|\Delta p|$ & same-head counterfactual sensitivity: change of the predicted probability when the artifact is inserted. \\
SMD & standardised mean difference between two groups. \\
\end{tabular}
